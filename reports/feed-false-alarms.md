# How often real weather repeats

Hours in the data: 17,544 (2011-01-01 to 2012-12-31, gaps included).

| Consecutive identical readings | Occurrences | Alerts per year at this threshold |
|---|---|---|
| 2 or more | 1227 | 613.5 |
| 3 or more | 99 | 49.5 |
| 4 or more | 24 | 12.0 |
| 5 or more | 3 | 1.5 |
| 6 or more | 1 | 0.5 |

Decision: the repeat count is charted, not paged. At 3 repeats it would page about 50 times a year on a healthy feed, and the age rule caught the frozen feed at the same tick with no false alarms.
