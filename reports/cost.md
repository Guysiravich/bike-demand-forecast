## 1. Estimate, made before running
Job: 0.5 vCPU / 1.0 GiB for 68 s a tick (feeder and forecast together,
measured on Container Apps: 32 s + 36 s, start to end of execution), 24 ticks a day.
Marginal cost of one run: **0.03390 THB** at list price. A month is 24,480 vCPU-seconds —
inside the free grant of 180,000, so the job itself
bills nothing while it is the only consumer.

## 3. The gap

**Fixed costs: estimate 11.3 THB a day, actual 11.2 — within 1%.** The registry (165 THB a month
estimated, 5.5 a day billed) and the VM's IP and disk were priced from the labs' own bills, which
is why they land.

**The project's own resources: estimated ~24 THB a month for hourly jobs, actual 0.** Two reasons,
both in our favour and both temporary: the jobs ran only during the drills and CD — about thirty
executions, not 24 a day — and every one of them fell inside the Container Apps monthly free
grant, as did Log Analytics' ingestion. Run hourly for a month, the jobs would use about 24,500
vCPU-seconds of the 180,000 free, so the bill for them would still be zero; the 24 THB in section
6 is the list-price value of that use, not a charge.

**The tracking server: estimated 317 THB a month at 24 hours a day, actual 0.14 USD (5 THB) in
October** — it ran a few hours on the deployment and drill days and is deallocated between
sessions. Left on for the demo week it would add about 10.5 THB a day; that, not the jobs, is the
cost to watch.

What the bill cannot show: the alert rules (about 0.10 USD each a month) bill at month end, so
they are not in a ten-day view yet.

## 6. One optimisation applied
| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly run on | Azure ML schedule, DS2_v2 cluster (the Lab 4 drift-job pattern) | Container Apps Job, 0.5 vCPU |
| Cost per run | ~1.2 THB (10 node-minutes from cold, course Lab 2 README) | 0.03390 THB |
| Per month at 24 runs a day | ~860 THB | 24.41 THB (inside the free grant) |

What it gave up: Azure ML's job history and lineage UI for inference runs. The forecasts and
the run log are in storage and Log Analytics instead.

## The next optimisation, not yet applied
The tracking-server VM is now the largest line, and only because the job reads the model from
it every hour. Copying the promoted version's artifact to blob storage at promotion time, and
loading it from there by version, would let the VM run only while training — and remove the
job's dependency on it (a single VM, a single point of failure for every run).
